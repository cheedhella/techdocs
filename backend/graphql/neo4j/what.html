1. What is Neo4j GraphQL Library?
  • With Neo4j GraphQL Library, you define your GraphQL schema and the library:
    • Auto-generates queries
    • Auto-generates mutations
    • Auto-generates filtering, sorting, pagination
    • Auto-generates relationship traversals
  • With the Neo4j GraphQL Library, you usually don’t write Cypher at all; You work at the GraphQL level, not the Cypher level;
  • You only write Cypher when you want custom graph logic that goes beyond standard CRUD and traversal;
2. New Terms
  • Cluster
    • A collection of one or more nodes working together;
  • Node
    • A single instance of Opensearch;
