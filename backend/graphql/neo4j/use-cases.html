1. Social Graphs
  • What It Is
    • Network of users and their relationships (friends, followers, connections);
  • Common Queries
    • Find friends of friends;
    • Suggest people you may know;
    • Calculate degrees of separation;
    • Find mutual connections;
  • Why GraphQL + Neo4j?
    • Efficiently traverse social connections;
    • Flexible queries for different views (timeline, profile, suggestions);
2. Knowledge Graphs
  • What It Is
    • Interconnected facts, entities, and concepts;
    • Examples: Wikipedia, Google Knowledge Graph;
  • Common Queries
    • Find all related concepts to a topic;
    • Discover paths between entities;
    • Answer complex questions by connecting facts;
  • Why GraphQL + Neo4j?
    • Model complex, evolving knowledge structures;
    • Query arbitrary depths of relationships;
    • Support semantic search and inference;
3. Recommendation Engines
  • What It Is
    • Systems that suggest items based on behavior and relationships;
  • Common Patterns
    • Collaborative filtering - "users like you also liked...";
    • Content-based - "similar to what you've viewed";
    • Graph-based - leverage network effects;
  • Why GraphQL + Neo4j?
    • Model users, items, and interactions as graph;
    • Real-time personalized recommendations;
    • Combine multiple signals (behavior, social, content);
