Backup and Restore
  • Export schema-only, data-only or full database backups;
  • Restore dumps into target instances for recovery or cloning;
  • Select specific objects to include in backup sets;
  • Use logical backups for portability across environments;
Reliable backup strategy
  • Take regular backups and verify restore on a test server;
  • Keep retention windows aligned with business requirements;
  • Protect backup files with encryption and access controls;
  • Version and timestamp backup artifacts consistently;
