1. What is Aggregate?
  • An Aggregate is a cluster of related objects treated as a single unit;
  • Eg: Order(root) -> OrderItems(children) and PaymentDetails(children);
2. What is the root of an Aggregate?
  • The root of an Aggregate is the object that is the main subject of the Aggregate;
3. What are the children of an Aggregate?
  • The children of an Aggregate are the objects that are related to the root object;
4. What is the purpose of an Aggregate?
  • The purpose of an Aggregate is to encapsulate the business logic for a related set of objects;
