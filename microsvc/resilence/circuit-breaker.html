What is Circuit Breaker?

  • We break down a monolitic application into several small services, that focus on a particular domain;
  • You don't feel any benefit from circuit breaker pattern if everything goes fine;
  • Let's say, service A depends on service B and service B is down; when client makes a call to service A, A makes a internal call to B; But, B is down; So, A will wait for B until timeout and throws an irritating 500 response to client;
  • Making the client read this error message, after waiting for long time is the most irritating thing a developer can do;

  • How are going to solve the issue? Are we going to fix B?
  • We are not going to fix B; But, what A can do is return the error message without making it's client wait;

  • Why is this important in microservices?
  • The risk of a service failing in a microservices architecture is very high with the number of independent services; If you have many services there is a huge chance for a service to fail;
  • On the other hand, when a service is down, if we continuously send requests to that service, it will make the things worse; So this pattern will reduce the load on the needed service;

  • Closed state
  • It means the service is up and running;
  • If the number of error responses from a service crosses a threshold limit, circuit breaker will goes to open stage;

  • Open state
  • It means that a service is not working properly, as requests failed more than the threshold;
  • In this state, circuit breaker will not make the remote calls;
  • With the circuit breakers, we implement a fallback method which will handle the case if the needed service is down;
  • So, in open state, circuit breaker will trigger the fallback method;
  • After a considerable time, the circuit breaker will go to half-open state;

  • Half-open state
  • In this stage, circuit breaker makes a remote call to the service;
  • If the request fails, the circuit breaker will go to the open stage;
  • If the service gives the proper response, the circuit breaker will go to the closed stage;

  • So, we can use the Circuit Breaker Pattern to improve the fault-tolerance and resilience of the Microservice Architecture and also to prevent the cascading of failure to other microservices;
