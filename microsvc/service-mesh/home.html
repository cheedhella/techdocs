Service Mesh

1. What is Service Mesh?
  • It is a dedicated infrastructure layer that manages service-to-service communication in a microservices architecture;
  • As the number of microservices grows, managing communication between them becomes increasingly complex due to issues like service discovery, load balancing, security, observability, and traffic management; This is where a Service Mesh comes into play;
2. Why Service Mesh?
    It acts like an intelligent traffic system that:
  • Controls the flow of vehicles (network requests) to prevent congestion;
  • Directs traffic efficiently (load balancing) to avoid bottlenecks;
  • Enforces traffic rules (security policies) to ensure safe driving;
  • Monitors road conditions (observability) and alerts authorities about accidents or jams;

            A service mesh typically uses sidecar proxies:

Each service gets a lightweight proxy deployed alongside it.

All network traffic goes through the proxy.

A central control plane manages policies and configuration.

Service A → Proxy A → Proxy B → Service B

The services don’t talk directly — the proxies handle communication.
