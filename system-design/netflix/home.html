1. What is Netflix System Design?
  • Designing a subscription-based video streaming service with a massive global reach;
  • Focuses on high availability, personalization, and content delivery;
2. Key Features
  • Content Ingestion: Preparing movies and shows for streaming;
  • Personalization: Sophisticated recommendation engine based on user behavior;
  • Open Connect: Netflix's own custom CDN for efficient content delivery;
  • Device Compatibility: Supporting thousands of different devices;
3. Technical Strategies
  • Microservices: Using thousands of services to manage different parts of the platform;
  • Chaos Engineering: Proactively testing system resilience by intentionally breaking things;
  • Global Traffic Management: Routing users to the best available data center or CDN node;
