1. What is AWS Organizations?
  • AWS Organizations helps you centrally manage and govern your environment as you grow and scale your AWS resources.
  • It enables you to centrally manage billing, control access, compliance, and security, and share resources across your AWS accounts.
2. What are Service Control Policies (SCPs)?
  • SCPs are a type of organization policy that you can use to manage permissions in your organization.
  • SCPs offer central control over the maximum available permissions for all accounts in your organization. They ensure your accounts stay within your organization's access control guidelines.
