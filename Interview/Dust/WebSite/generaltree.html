Representation of General trees:

Each node contains some data, pointer to it's 1st child and pointer to it's 1st sibling.

  struct tree_node
  {
  element_type element;
  tree_ptr first_child;
  tree_ptr next_sibling;
  };

Representation of General trees:
preorder traversal:   work at a node is performed before (pre) its children are processed. Eg: ls -R list recursively
postorder traversal:  work at a node is performed after (post) its children are evaluated. Eg: ls -s size of dir
nothing like inorder for general trees.
