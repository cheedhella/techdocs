What is Bridge Pattern?

It can be thought of as 2 layers of abstraction.
It decouples an abstraction from it's implementation so that 2 classes can vary independantly. 
In short, it converts 'is-a relationship' to 'has-a relationship'.
It helps us to avoid ending up with multiple concrete classes that will be exposed to the client.
