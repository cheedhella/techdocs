1. New Terms
         Generative AI comes in several types, each defined by how the model learns and what kind of content it generates. Below is a clear, organized list of the main types, with simple explanations and examples.

✅ 1. Large Language Models (LLMs)

These models generate text and sometimes code, reasoning, and conversation.

Examples:

GPT (ChatGPT)

Google Gemini

Anthropic Claude

LLaMA

What they generate:

Text, chats, summaries

Code

Answers to questions

✅ 2. Diffusion Models

These models generate high-quality images, video, or audio by gradually turning random noise into a structured output.

Examples:

DALL·E

Stable Diffusion

Midjourney

Imagen

What they generate:

Photorealistic images

Digital art

Videos (newer diffusion models)

✅ 3. Generative Adversarial Networks (GANs)

GANs use a generator and a discriminator competing with each other to produce realistic outputs.

Examples:

StyleGAN

CycleGAN

What they generate:

Realistic faces

Deepfakes

Image style transfer

Synthetic images for training datasets

✅ 4. Variational Autoencoders (VAEs)

VAEs learn a compressed representation of data and then decode it to generate new variations.

What they generate:

Images

Latent space manipulations (smooth edits)

Creative variations of existing designs

Often used when smoother or controlled generation is needed.

✅ 5. Autoregressive Models

These models generate output one step at a time based on previous steps.

Examples:

GPT (text)

PixelRNN / PixelCNN (images)

Wavenet (audio)

What they generate:

Text sequences

Pixel-by-pixel images

Audio waveforms

✅ 6. Transformers for Multimodal Generation

These models handle multiple types of input/output at once.

Examples:

GPT-4o (multimodal)

Gemini Ultra

LLaVA

What they generate:

Text + images

Code + voice

Image analysis + generation

✅ 7. Audio Generative Models

Models specialized for sound generation.

Examples:

AudioLM

VALL-E

MusicLM

Jukebox

What they generate:

Music

Sound effects

Voice synthesis

Speech from text (TTS systems)

✅ 8. Video Generative Models

These models generate video frames over time.

Examples:

Sora by OpenAI

Runway Gen-2

Pika Labs

What they generate:

Short video clips

Animations

Motion sequences

✅ 9. 3D Generative Models

These models create 3D structures, objects, or environments.

Examples:

3D diffusion models

NeRF (Neural Radiance Fields)

Point-E

What they generate:

3D shapes

Product prototypes

Virtual worlds for games/VR


| Type                  | What it Generates               | Examples         |
| --------------------- | ------------------------------- | ---------------- |
| LLMs                  | Text, code, dialogue            | GPT, Claude      |
| Diffusion models      | Images, video                   | Stable Diffusion |
| GANs                  | Realistic images, deepfakes     | StyleGAN         |
| VAEs                  | Smooth latent-controlled images | VAE              |
| Autoregressive models | Text, audio, images             | GPT, WaveNet     |
