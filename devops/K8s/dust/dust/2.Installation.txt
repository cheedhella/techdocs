K8s Installation
	Step 1: k8sversion=`curl -s https://storage.googleapis.com/kubernetes-release/release/stable.txt`
	Step 2: curl -LO https://storage.googleapis.com/kubernetes-release/release/$k8sversion/bin/darwin/amd64/kubectl
	Step 3: chmod +x ./kubectl
	Step 4: sudo mv ./kubectl /usr/local/bin/kubectl
