Kubernetes Scheduler Overview

1. What is the Scheduler?
  • A control plane component that assigns Pods to Nodes;
  • Watches for newly created Pods that have no node assigned (spec.nodeName is empty);
  • Finds the best node for each Pod based on constraints and available resources;
2. Scheduling Process
  • Step 1 — Filtering: Eliminate nodes that don't meet the Pod's requirements (insufficient resources, taints, affinity rules);
  • Step 2 — Scoring: Rank the remaining nodes based on preferences (spread, affinity, resource balance);
  • Step 3 — Binding: Assign the Pod to the highest-scored node;
3. Scheduling Factors
FactorDescription
Resource requestsDoes the node have enough CPU/memory?
nodeSelectorDoes the node have the required labels?
Node AffinityPrefer or require specific nodes
Pod Affinity/Anti-AffinityCo-locate or spread Pods
Taints and TolerationsNode repels Pods unless they tolerate the taint
Topology SpreadDistribute Pods across failure domains
4. Manual Scheduling
  • You can bypass the scheduler by setting spec.nodeName directly;
apiVersion: v1
kind: Pod
metadata:
  name: manual-pod
spec:
  nodeName: worker-2
  containers:
    - name: nginx
      image: nginx
