Volume

1. What is a Volume?
  • Volume is a directory/storage that is attached to the Pod, not the containers;
  • It is defined at the Pod level and mounted into containers;
  • It allows containers in a pod to store and share data;
  • So, data in the Volume survives even if the container is restarted or deleted;
  • Data may persist permanently, if the Volume is attached to a Persistent Volume;
2. Type of Volumes?
  • Ephemeral Volumes - Short-lived volumes that are created and destroyed with the Pod;
    • emptyDir
    • hostPath
  • Persistent Volume
