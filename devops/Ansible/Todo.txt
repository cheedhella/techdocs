Logging 
    - Ansible Logs 
        - Location of logs: <playbook-home>/ansible_ifm.log
        - Archived logs: <playbook-home>/logs/ansible_ifm<date>.log
        - Changing log level -
            -v : Verbose    (Default)
            -vv : More verbose
            -vvv : Very verbose
            -vvvv : Debugging level 
    - Service Logs 
        - Location: <service-dir>/logs/topology-service.log
        - Archive: <service-dir>/archived/topology-service<date>.log
        - Changing log level: <service-dir>/logback.xml 
            - Log levels: TRACE, DEBUG, INFO, WARN and ERROR
            - Advice is to keep the root logger always INFO level;


        Changing log level 
           -- Every service has a logback.xml 
           -- <logger name="com.broadcom.netops" level="INFO" />
        
Neo4j 
    - start/stop logs(<NEO4J_HOME>/logs/neo4j.log)
    - runtime logs(<NEO4J_HOME>/logs/debug.log)
    - Changing log level: <NEO4J_HOME>/conf/neo4j.conf      # Requires restart of neo4j 
        dbms.logs.debug.level=INFO
