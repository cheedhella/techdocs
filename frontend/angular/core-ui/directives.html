Directives (Structural and Attribute)
  • Use structural directives to add or remove DOM fragments.
  • Use attribute directives to change appearance or behavior of elements.
  • Understand built-in directives such as ngIf, ngFor, and ngClass.
  • Build custom directives for shared interactions and visual states.
  • Keep directive logic focused and reusable across components.
  • Test directives to verify DOM behavior under changing inputs.
